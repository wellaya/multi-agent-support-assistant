# Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account from Account Settings > Privacy >
Delete Account. This requires re-entering your password to confirm. Once
submitted, deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved preferences, and
uploaded content. It does not automatically cancel an active paid
subscription — cancel your subscription first under Account Settings >
Billing to avoid being charged again before deleting.

We retain minimal billing records for legal and tax purposes for up to 7
years after account deletion, but this data is not linked to your
deleted profile and is not used for any other purpose.

If you deleted your account by mistake, contact support immediately.
Accounts can only be restored within 48 hours of deletion, after which
the data is permanently purged.
