# Billing FAQ

Your subscription renews automatically each month on the date you first
signed up, unless you cancel before the renewal date. You can view your
next billing date under Account Settings > Billing.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. To update your
payment method, go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods and
add a new card before removing the old one.

If a payment fails, we'll retry it three times over five days. You'll get
an email notification after each failed attempt. If all retries fail,
your account will be downgraded to the free tier, but your data is kept
for 30 days in case you want to resubscribe.

Invoices are available as PDF downloads under Account Settings > Billing >
Invoice History, and are also emailed automatically after each successful
charge.
