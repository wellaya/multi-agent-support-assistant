# Resetting Your Password

If you've forgotten your password, go to the login page and click
"Forgot password?". Enter the email address associated with your account
and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes.

If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam
folder. If it's still not there, make sure you're using the same email
address you originally signed up with.

Once you click the reset link, you'll be asked to choose a new password.
Passwords must be at least 8 characters and include a number and a
special character. After resetting, you'll be signed out of all other
active sessions for security.

If you no longer have access to the email on your account, contact
support with your account username and we'll verify your identity
manually before updating your email.
