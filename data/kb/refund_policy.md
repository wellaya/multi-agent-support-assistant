# Refund Policy

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your original purchase date, no
questions asked. To request one, contact support with your order number
and we'll process it within 3-5 business days back to your original
payment method.

After 14 days, refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis, generally
only for unused annual plans or in the case of a billing error on our
side. Partial-month refunds for monthly plans are not offered outside the
14-day window; instead you can cancel to stop future charges.

Refunds for one-time purchases (like add-on credits) follow the same
14-day window, but unused credits do not roll over once a refund is
issued.

If a refund doesn't appear in your account within 10 business days after
we've confirmed it was processed, please reach back out and reference
your original support ticket number.
