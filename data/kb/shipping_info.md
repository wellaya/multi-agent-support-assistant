# Shipping Information

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the country of purchase.
Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
additional fee. We currently ship to the US, Canada, UK, and Australia.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Tracking
can take up to 24 hours to update after it's first issued, so don't worry
if it looks inactive right away.

If your package is marked as delivered but you haven't received it, wait
24 hours (carriers sometimes mark packages delivered slightly early),
check with neighbors and your building's front desk, and if it still
hasn't turned up, contact support with your order number so we can file a
claim with the carrier.

We are not able to redirect a package to a different address once it has
shipped. If you need to change the delivery address, contact support
before the order ships.
